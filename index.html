About Us

“

TIS Phantom Shadow had a demanding and unforgettable 2025 season, earning the opportunity to compete at both Regionals and Nationals through months of hard work, innovation, and teamwork. Reaching those competitions was a major achievement, but the journey was far from easy. At both events, the team faced setbacks — the robot did not always perform as planned, the pressure was high, and unexpected problems arose at critical moments. These challenges were frustrating, but they pushed the team to think deeper, adapt faster, and support one another through difficult situations. Rather than viewing those moments as defeats, TIS Phantom Shadow chose to treat them as valuable lessons. The 2025 season revealed important areas for improvement in preparation, strategy, and execution. With renewed motivation and a clearer vision, TIS Phantom Shadow has decided to return in 2026 stronger than ever, ready to learn from the past, sharpen their skills, and strive for greater success together.

Innovation Project

WaiCheck

A handheld, solar-powered river safety scanner built for everyday explorers, swimmers, fishers, and wildlife protection.

WaiCheck Innovation Demo

Testing river purity, bacteria, pollution, and temperature in about 40 seconds before anyone steps into the water.

Detect · Alert · Respond

Water Safety for People & Wildlife

When visiting a river for the first time, it is hard to tell whether the water is safe to swim in, healthy for native fish, or clean enough for wildlife to drink. Existing testing equipment is slow, expensive, and built for councils rather than everyday families and kids.

WaiCheck solves this with an affordable, welded metal handheld device powered by top-mounted solar panels with a 10-day battery and zero gas. Simply hold WaiCheck half-submerged in slow-flowing water for ~20 seconds, and within ~40 seconds total the screen alerts you with clear river safety readings.

~40s Total Scan Time
10 Days Solar Battery Life
3 Checks Pollution · Bacteria · Temp
01 · Problem

Identify & Research

Connor and Kian identified the river safety challenge, and the entire team studied water pollution, bacteria, and temperature impacts.

02 · Discovery

Survey & Solution

Nathaniel and Tehem evaluated existing testing tools while Kevin and Liam surveyed people on how water safety affects their community.

03 · Fabrication

Welded Build & Electronics

Instead of basic LEGO, blueprints were taken to Palmerston North to weld the casing, while Liam, Kevin, Kian, and Connor built and wired the sensors.

04 · Iteration

Test, Solar & Share

Connor and Nathaniel tested and refined prototypes, added solar panels for the 10-day battery from user feedback, and shared WaiCheck with the community.

Research Sources: Yawawawa · Hygger · World Health Organization (who.int)
